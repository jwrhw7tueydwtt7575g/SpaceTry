How the safety filter works

The pilot is not trusted. It can be the glideslope controller, the PPO policy, a direct-thrust law or a person on the keyboard. Before each 1 s step, the v2 filter predicts the motion under the pilot's command and checks the closing speed against a limit that depends on range. If the step stays under the limit, the command goes through unchanged. If not, the glideslope controller flies that step, or a brake if the glideslope step would also break the limit. This is the Simplex pattern.

Off

Every command reaches the thrusters.

v1 veto

Inside 5 m and above 0.2 m/s, any command that does not reduce speed is replaced by zero thrust. The craft keeps coasting at the speed it had.

v2 filter

Outside 5 m the limit is a braking curve that reaches 0.2 m/s at the 5 m sphere; inside it tapers to 0.05 m/s at capture.
