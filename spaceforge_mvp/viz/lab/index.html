Let anything fly. Never let it hit the station.

SpaceForge treats every pilot (a learned policy, a planner, a human on a joystick) as untrusted. A runtime-assurance filter predicts each control step against a closing-speed envelope and, only when the step would breach it, hands authority to a certified backup law. This is the Simplex architecture.

Off

Whatever the pilot commands reaches the thrusters. The reckless law strikes the station at over 1 m/s.

v1 · veto

The legacy interlock can only say no, and a veto means zero thrust, so a fast vehicle still coasts into the target.

v2 · runtime assurance

Predictive and minimal: the pilot keeps the stick whenever the step is safe; otherwise the certified backup flies that step.

The browser simulation is a port of the Python environment (exact Clohessy-Wiltshire transition matrix) and is checked against Python-generated fixtures in CI. The PPO pilot is the shipped checkpoint, running in plain JavaScript.
